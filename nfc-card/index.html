Print & NFC notes

  • Size: CR80 credit-card, 85.6 × 54 mm, ~3.4 mm corner radius. For a print shop, add 2–3 mm bleed on every side and keep text ≥4 mm from the edge (already respected here).
  • Tap zone: the center ring marks where the NFC chip sits — avoid metallic/foil finishes over the center, as metal detunes the antenna. Matte or soft-touch over the center prints fine.
  • QR: points to https://profile.trideep.co.in on a white tile for reliable scanning. Regenerate at higher resolution for the final print if needed.
  • Encode the chip (NTAG213/215) with the same URL using any “NFC Tools” app so a tap opens your profile.
  • Export: use Print → Save as PDF (this page prints each face true-size, one per page) and hand that to the printer.
