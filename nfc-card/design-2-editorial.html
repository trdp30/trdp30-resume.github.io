Print & NFC notes

  • Size: CR80, 85.6 × 54 mm, ~3.4 mm radius. Add 2–3 mm bleed for the printer.
  • Finish: this one shines on uncoated / soft-touch stock with the accent bar in spot gloss or foil. Keep the center (chip) free of metal foil.
  • QR → profile.trideep.co.in, on a white chip for reliable scanning. Encode the NTAG chip with the same URL.
  • Export: Print → Save as PDF (true size, one card per page).
